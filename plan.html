RPE — what the numbers mean

RPE is how far you stopped from failure, not how heavy the weight is.

100 reps left — total failure
91 rep left
82 reps left
73 reps left

Heavy compounds → stop at RPE 8. Isolation → RPE 9. The only RPE 10 all week is Saturday's lateral raise drop set.

Flex day rules
  • 150 g protein floor — non-negotiable.
  • One unbudgeted meal, not a whole unbudgeted day.
  • Ceiling ~3,500 kcal. You won't count it, you'll feel it.
  • Alcohol comes out of the flex budget, not on top of it.

Two blowout days erases the entire week's deficit. This is the single biggest failure point in the plan.

Tracking & adjustment

Weigh every morning, use the 7-day rolling average only. Tape waist / shoulders / calves Monday mornings. Photos every 2 weeks.

Over 2 weeksDo this
Lost 0.5–0.7 kgOn track — change nothing
Lost >0.9 kgToo fast — add 150 kcal carbs
Lost <0.25 kg, waist flatStalled — one rung of cardio
Lost <0.25 kg, waist downRecomping — change nothing
Strength down 2 wksEat more or add a rest day

Never cut calories and add cardio in the same adjustment.

Cardio ladder — climb one rung at a time
  • Steps 10k → 12k
  • Second zone-2 bike (Tue or Fri, 30 min, after lifting)
  • Sunday bike → 75 min
  • 15 min incline walk after two lifting sessions

Only when genuinely stalled. Adding cardio beats cutting calories — it doesn't compromise recovery or training.
